## Production requirements
- These are selected candidates, not yet downloaded or committed. The MP4 files must be added before the live video backgrounds can play.
- Download each clip from its Pexels page; do not hotlink or embed a third-party player.
- Confirm the current clip-specific licence and inspect the footage for visible third-party logos, trademarks, identifiable people and other rights before publication.
- Prefer landscape 16:9, H.264 MP4, 24–30 fps.
- Trim to a seamless 8–15 second loop where practical; remove audio; compress for web delivery.
- Keep a record of the source page, creator, download date and licence with the project assets.

Pexels states that its videos may be used in commercial projects without attribution. That licence does not automatically clear separate rights in depicted brands, trademarks or people. See the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and [commercial-use guidance](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042295214-Can-I-use-the-photos-and-videos-for-a-commercial-project).
